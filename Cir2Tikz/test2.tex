The lemma \texttt{lemma-S$\downarrow$HCZH} in \texttt{N/Ex.agda} (line 100) states:
\[
  S \cdot H \cdot CZ \cdot H \;\equiv_s\; H \cdot CZ \cdot H \cdot CZ \cdot S^{\uparrow} \cdot S
\]
where $\cdot$ is matrix multiplication order (rightmost applied first to the state), wire $0$ is bottom ($\downarrow$), wire $1$ is top ($\uparrow$).

%% -----------------------------------------------------------------------
\section*{Main theorem}
%% -----------------------------------------------------------------------

\[
  S \cdot H \cdot CZ \cdot H \;\equiv_s\; H \cdot CZ \cdot H \cdot CZ \cdot S^{\uparrow} \cdot S
\]
\scalebox{0.85}{\tikzfig{shczh}}

%% -----------------------------------------------------------------------
\section*{Proof chain}
%% -----------------------------------------------------------------------

The proof proceeds from the right-hand side $H \cdot CZ \cdot H \cdot CZ \cdot S^{\uparrow} \cdot S$
to the left-hand side $S \cdot H \cdot CZ \cdot H$ in two main steps:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Apply \textbf{selinger-c11} to the inner $CZ \cdot H \cdot CZ$, then cancel
        $S^{-1\uparrow} \cdot S^{\uparrow} = \varepsilon$ and $S^{-1} \cdot S = \varepsilon$.
  \item Apply the Hadamard conjugation $H \cdot S^{-1} \cdot H \equiv_s S \cdot H \cdot S$
        to the leftmost triple, then cancel $S \cdot S^{-1} = \varepsilon$.
\end{enumerate}
